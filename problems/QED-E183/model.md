# QED-E183 — Is AK(3) Andrews–Curtis trivial?

## The problem
Extended set · Grade A · QED Curated · Algebra

**Plain words:** The presentation <x, y | x^3 = y^4, xyx = yxy> defines the trivial group. Can its two relators be transformed into the trivial pair (x, y) using only the elementary moves: multiply one relator by the other or its inverse, invert a relator, or conjugate a relator? It is known that this works if one extra generator and relator may be added, but without that help nobody has found a sequence.

**Math:** In $F_2=F(x,y)$ let $r_1=xyxy^{-1}x^{-1}y^{-1}$, $r_2=x^3y^{-4}$. AC-moves on pairs $(u,v)$: $(u,v)\mapsto(uv^{\pm1},v)$, $(u^{-1},v)$, $(gug^{-1},v)$ for $g\in F_2$, the same with roles swapped, and $(u,v)\mapsto(v,u)$. Is $(r_1,r_2)$ in the equivalence class of $(x,y)$ under the equivalence relation generated by AC-moves?
