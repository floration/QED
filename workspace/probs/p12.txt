## The spectre is not spectral | 7 | Analysis
W: Show the spectre, a curved tile that cannot tile by translations, has no orthogonal basis of exponentials, as Fuglede's conjecture predicts.
M: Prove there is no $\Lambda\subset\mathbb{R}^2$ such that $\{e^{2\pi i\lambda\cdot x}\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ is an orthogonal basis of $L^2$ of the spectre domain.

## Midpoint-prime orbit lengths | 6 | Dynamics
W: Find the average number of steps the midpoint-prime map takes to reach 1, on a logarithmic scale.
M: For the midpoint-prime map $m$, prove $\frac1x\sum_{n\le x}\frac{T_m(n)}{\log n}\to c$ and compute $c$, where $T_m(n)=\min\{k:m^k(n)=1\}$.
