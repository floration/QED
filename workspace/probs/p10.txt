## Affine prime maps all settle | 8 | Dynamics
W: Fix whole numbers a and b. Send a prime p to the largest prime factor of ap + b. Every case tested falls into a loop. Prove this always happens.
M: Prove that for all fixed integers $a\ge2$, $b\ge1$, every orbit of $p\mapsto P(ap+b)$ on primes is eventually periodic.

## Affine prime maps, quantitative | 6 | Dynamics

M: For $p\mapsto P(3p+4)$, prove that for all but $o(\pi(x))$ primes $p\le x$ the orbit of $p$ enters its (unique observed) 7-cycle within $C\log\log p$ steps.
