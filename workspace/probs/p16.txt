M: Let $\delta_G$ be the translative-and-rotational packing density of Gerver's sofa. Prove $\delta_G\notin\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$.

## Distance sets of the spectre tiling | 6 | Geometry

M: Determine the order of growth of $\#\{|x-y|:x,y\in\Lambda_{\mathrm{sp}},|x|,|y|\le R\}$.

## Unforced blow-up near the forced one | 9 | PDE
W: Show the known forced Navier–Stokes blow-up can be made to work with forcing that decays to zero at the blow-up time.
M: Prove there exist smooth $u_0$ and $f\in L^1_tL^2_x\cap C^\infty$ with $\|f(t)\|_{L^2}\to0$ as $t\to T^-$ such that the forced 3D NS solution blows up at $T$.

## Isospectral monotiles | 6 | Geometry

## Prime-digit continued fractions in arithmetic progressions | 7 | Number theory
